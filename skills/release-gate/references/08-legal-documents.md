# Phase 8: Legal documents and policy consistency

## Goal
(a) Check any existing policy text against what the code does. (b) Generate DRAFT legal documents from the confirmed data inventory so every statement traces to an inventory entry. Never present either as legal advice.

## Rules (SKILL principle 4)
- Every generated document starts with: "DRAFT generated by release-gate. Not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review before publishing, especially if you process payments, health data, children's data, or operate in regulated industries." The templates already carry the banner; keep it.
- Law references come only from `references/law/*.yaml`. Otherwise write: "not covered by this skill's reference data; verify with a lawyer or official source". Flag entries with `last_verified` older than 12 months as possibly stale.
- Traceability: a statement with no inventory entry or config answer is removed. An inventory entry with no statement is a reported gap.
- No invented retention periods, security measures, training promises or legal entities. Missing value: leave `{{TODO ...}}` and say so.
- Write output to `<project>/.release-gate/documents/`. Do not write into the project tree or commit anything unless the user asks.
- Always finish with the questions only a lawyer can answer.

## Prerequisites
- `data-inventory.yaml` (phase 1). If `confirmed_by_user` is false, say it in the first line of every document's gap list and in the report; offer to confirm it now.
- `config.yaml` (phase 0): `operator.*`, `audience.*`, `features.*`, `product.distribution`.
- Phase 7 results (`privacy.json`): rights tests and cookie audit change what the documents may promise.

## Step 1: Policy consistency (LEGAL-POLICY-CONSISTENCY, quick/full/legal)
Needs an existing policy file (txt/html/md). None exists: skip to step 2, and record `NOT-TESTED: no existing policy to check` (generated drafts are checked in step 3).
```
python <skill>/scripts/policy_consistency.py --project <project> \
  --policy <path/to/privacy-policy> [--policy <another>] \
  [--inventory <project>/.release-gate/data-inventory.yaml] [--out <path>]
# default output: <project>/.release-gate/policy_findings.json
```
Reads the claim text, so a truthful policy that discloses analytics is not flagged. Claims conditioned on consent ("only after you accept") are skipped.

| Policy claim | Contradicted by inventory | Entry written |
|---|---|---|
| "we do not share/sell data with third parties" | a telemetry/analytics/subprocessor destination | `TOOL-CONFIRMED` high, evidence = policy `file:line` + inventory refs |
| "we do not collect personal data" | any personal field in the inventory | `TOOL-CONFIRMED` high |
| "we do not track / no analytics / no cookies" | analytics storage or telemetry destinations | `SUSPECTED` high until step 1b |
| "we delete your data / account" | no `rights.deletion` in the inventory | `SUSPECTED` high until step 1b |
| "data is encrypted at rest" | no store marked `encrypted_at_rest: yes` | `SUSPECTED` medium |

All entries use `check_id: LEGAL-POLICY-CONSISTENCY`, `category: policy-mismatch`, ids `RG-POLICYMISMATCH-nnn`.

**1b. Runtime confirmation** (needs the phase-3 app and scope):
- tracking claim: run the phase-7 cookie audit. Tracker before consent or after reject: upgrade the entry to `EXPLOITED`, attach `cookie-audit.json`. Cookie audit clean: mark `status: false-positive` with `verification.reason` citing the audit.
- deletion claim: run the phase-7 deletion test. Data remains or login still works: `EXPLOITED`. Deleted correctly: `false-positive` with reason and evidence.
- Cannot run: leave `SUSPECTED` and set `not_tested_reason` to why (`application not running`, `out of scope: <reason>`).

Verify every `TOOL-CONFIRMED` yourself: open the policy line and the inventory refs (`verification.code_ref`). A mismatch you cannot reproduce from the cited lines is a false positive.

No mismatch found: `VERIFIED-ABSENT`, title "policy_consistency found no contradiction between <policy file> and the inventory (claims matched: no-share, no-collect, no-track, deletion, encryption)". It checks those five claim types only; any other claim in the policy is unchecked, list them.

Merge into the main file: `dedupe_findings.py` on the concatenation, or pass `--findings <project>/.release-gate/policy_findings.json` to `report_build.py`.

## Step 2: Generate drafts (LEGAL-DOCS-GENERATED, full/legal)
Templates are in `templates/legal/*.tmpl`; selection table and placeholder syntax are in `templates/legal/README.md`. Read it first.
1. Pick documents from config and inventory (examples): privacy policy when any personal data exists; terms of service for website/saas/api/mobile-app or accounts; EULA for desktop-app/game/game-mod/mobile-app/self-hosted/cli; cookie policy and consent UI text when non-strictly-necessary `client_storage` exists; subprocessor list when third parties exist; AI disclosure when `features.ai_features`; refund policy when payments/subscriptions; mod disclaimer for `game-mod`; security.txt / vulnerability disclosure policy for internet-facing services; accessibility statement for user-facing UI; third-party notices for distributed dependencies.
2. Fill `{{UPPER_CASE}}` from `config.yaml` or chat answers; expand `{{#fields}}` / `{{#third_parties}}` / `{{#client_storage}}` / `{{#stores}}` loops from the inventory; render conditionals from `features.*` and inventory-derived flags. `rights.*` decides which rights may be promised (no `rights.deletion` means do not write "you can delete your data").
3. Regional sections come from the applicable regimes in `privacy.json` / `references/law/*.yaml` (for example GDPR sections only if GDPR may apply; COPPA when `under_13_possible` or `directed_at_children`). Cite the file's `source_ref`, not memory.
4. Leave `{{TODO ...}}` and `{{LAWYER_QUESTIONS}}` unresolved where the user has not answered; never fill by guessing. Remove the `<!-- AUTHOR NOTES -->` block only when the user publishes.
5. Save each file under `.release-gate/documents/` and list a gap table: inventory entry with no statement, statement with no entry (removed), unresolved TODOs.

## Step 3: Check the drafts
Run the consistency check on your own drafts, to confirm the generator did not contradict the inventory:
```
python <skill>/scripts/policy_consistency.py --project <project> --policy .release-gate/documents/privacy-policy.md
```
A draft that triggers a mismatch is a bug in your fill; fix it, do not report it as a product finding. Record: `LEGAL-DOCS-GENERATED` check (evidence: the files under `documents/`, paths relative to `.release-gate/`) with `VERIFIED-ABSENT` as completed marker only when every applicable document was produced and the self-check was clean; title "N drafts generated from inventory; self-consistency check clean; not reviewed by a lawyer". Some applicable documents not produced: `SUSPECTED` with the list. None: `NOT-TESTED: <reason>`.

## documents.json (read by `report_build.py`)
```json
{
  "documents": [{"name": "Privacy policy", "path": ".release-gate/documents/privacy-policy.md",
                 "applies_reason": "personal data in 7 inventory fields"}],
  "lawyer_questions": ["Is {{ENTITY}} a controller or processor for data sent to <vendor>?",
                       "Which regime governs <region> users? (not covered by this skill's reference data)"]
}
```
Lawyer questions: gather each applicable regime's `lawyer_questions`, plus project-specific ones (international transfers, children, sector rules, DPO/representative need, consent wording, ToS limits, mod/EULA/IP rights, payments). Report prints them in section 6.

## Adversarial verification
No critical-category check here. A High policy-mismatch finding still needs it (SKILL principle 2): give a fresh subagent the policy line and the inventory refs and tell it to disprove the contradiction (conditional wording, different data, first-party host). For `EXPLOITED` upgrades, have it re-run the audit or deletion test. Refuted: mark false-positive with the reason.

## NOT-TESTED strings (exact)
- `no existing policy to check`
- `no data inventory` (documents are generated only from a confirmed or clearly flagged inventory)
- `application not running` / `out of scope: <reason>` (step 1b)
- `operator details not provided` (documents produced with TODOs; list them)
- `skipped by user`
